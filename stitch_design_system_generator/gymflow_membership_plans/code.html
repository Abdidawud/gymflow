Membership Rules & Front-Desk Policy

Key operational guarantees enforced across all active branches.

verified_user
calendar_month Fixed Calendar Days

Durations are computed as rigid integers (e.g., 30, 90, 365 days) rather than variable astrological months to avoid renewal ambiguity in February or leap years.

currency_exchange Non-Retroactive Adjustments

Price updates never recalculate ongoing contracts or existing payments. A member who paid 1,500 ETB retains full term access without surcharge.

delete_forever Zero Record Deletion

In compliance with accounting audits, plans cannot be deleted once assigned to a member. Unwanted tiers are archived via the Disable toggle.
